Excel Data Cleaning & Quality Control

A structured approach to making a messy customer-order export dependable enough for analytics, reporting, and downstream automation.

ExcelValidationData Cleaning

The business problem

A 726-record order export contained inconsistent names, conflicting order records, duplicate entries, mixed date and currency formats, missing values, and malformed email addresses. These issues undermined reliable filtering and reporting.

What I built

I preserved the raw source and developed a cleaned master dataset with standardized identifiers, dates, customer names, categories, statuses, and amounts. A separate cleaning log documented changes; an exceptions list preserved unresolved items for review.

My method

I used structured Excel tables, XLOOKUP, TRIM, PROPER, SUBSTITUTE, IF/IFERROR, conditional formatting, filters, duplicate analysis, and data validation. Standardization rules were kept visible and auditable.

Quality controls

I did not silently guess at conflicting values. Ambiguous records went to an exception list so a reviewer could make a business decision without losing the source record.

Business value

A cleaner export reduces manual rework and makes follow-on reports, PivotTables, dashboarding, and automation more trustworthy.
