Operations Performance & Business Analytics

An operations analytics portfolio project for a fictional regional distributor, turning raw transactional data into actionable executive and operational reporting.

TableauExcelKPI Design

The business problem

Leadership needed one clear picture of revenue, customer concentration, fulfillment reliability, cancellations, and returns. Raw order exports made it hard to spot meaningful trends or distinguish changes in business activity from differences in record grain.

What I built

I audited the dataset and created Tableau reporting views for Executive Overview, Sales & Customer Analysis, and Operations & Fulfillment. The work included KPI definitions, filters, calculated fields, and drill-down analysis designed around management questions.

Key analytical decisions

Because orders could have multiple product-line records, I treated order counts differently from line-level return metrics. I checked missing values, duplicates, category consistency, date ranges, and revenue logic before reporting.

Insights and recommendations

The fictional case study highlighted a customer retention risk at Northstar Health Systems, strong recent revenue growth in the Northeast, and a South-region delivery performance gap. It also identified a change toward online ordering, a temporary cancellation spike, and a relatively high technology return rate.

Business value

Demonstrates an end-to-end analytics process that turns raw operations exports into understandable performance measures and targeted questions leadership can investigate.

Scope and transparency

All companies, customer names, transactions, and figures in this portfolio dataset are fictional. This is a demonstration project, not a real client engagement.
