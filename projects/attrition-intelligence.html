Academic capstone · Concept/design

Employee Attrition & Engagement Intelligence

Academic capstone design for a privacy-conscious platform that connects employee feedback with sentiment, retention risk signals, and role-appropriate reporting.

SQL ServerNLPPower BI

The business problem

Traditional annual surveys can miss early warning signs of disengagement. HR teams need a way to identify patterns without exposing sensitive individual feedback to managers or weakening trust in the survey process.

Proposed solution

The project designed a conversational survey intake, an anonymization and ETL flow, NLP-based sentiment enrichment, an attrition-risk model, and distinct reporting experiences for HR and executives.

Data architecture

The design uses SQL Server data warehousing and SSIS-style processing. Survey data, HR/behavioral attributes, sentiment results, and modeled risk signals feed the reporting layer through controlled transformations.

Privacy and access decisions

Managers are restricted to survey participation and engagement only, rather than comments, sentiment, or individual risk scores. A minimum response threshold prevents small-team results from being displayed when anonymity could be compromised.

What it demonstrates

Requirements analysis, dimensional modeling, analytics pipeline design, predictive analytics concepts, role-based authorization, and practical consideration of privacy tradeoffs.

Scope and transparency

Academic design/capstone — not a claim that a production HR platform has been deployed or that risk predictions have been validated in a real workforce.
